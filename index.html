Real-time Danish transit departures for bus, metro, S-tog and train — right on your iPhone.

Features

  • Live departures See real-time departures from your favorite stations, powered by Rejseplanen.
  • Map search Browse stops on a map and add them to your favorites with one tap.
  • Commute schedules Set up morning and evening schedules — the app shows the right lines at the right time.
  • Widgets & Live Activity See the next departure from your home screen, lock screen, or Dynamic Island.
  • Works offline Cached departures keep widgets up to date even when the app is in the background.
