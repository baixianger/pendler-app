1.0.1build 2

April 2026
  • Fixed a critical connectivity issue The app was unable to load departures after its initial release. This is now fully resolved — the real-time departure board loads reliably again.
  • New offline mode When you lose signal, Pendler now shows saved departures from the same type of day (weekday, Saturday, or Sunday), shifted to today's schedule. A banner at the top of the list tells you when you're viewing cached data.
  • Clear offline cache Under Settings → Storage, you can now wipe all saved departures with a single tap — useful if you want to free up space or force the app to fetch fresh data.
  • Rettet forbindelsesproblem Appen kunne ikke hente afgange efter udgivelse. Det er nu fuldstændig løst — tavlen henter igen afgange i realtid.
  • Ny offlinetilstand Hvis du mister forbindelse undervejs, viser Pendler nu gemte afgange fra samme type dag (hverdag, lørdag eller søndag) tilpasset dagens tidspunkter. Et banner øverst på listen viser, når du ser gemte data.
  • Ryd offlinecache Under Indstillinger → Lager kan du nu slette alle gemte afgange med ét tryk — nyttigt, hvis du vil frigøre plads eller tvinge appen til at hente friske data.
  • 修复连接问题 修复了应用发布后无法加载班次信息的问题,实时班次表现在可以正常加载。
  • 新增离线模式 网络中断时,Pendler 会根据同类型日期(工作日、周六或周日)显示本地缓存的班次,并自动调整为今天的时间。 列表顶部的提示条会告诉你当前显示的是缓存数据。
  • 清除离线缓存 在 设置 → 存储 中,一键即可清除所有本地缓存的班次数据 — 适合需要释放存储空间,或希望强制刷新数据时使用。
More releases will appear here as they ship.
